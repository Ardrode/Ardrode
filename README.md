### Hi there, I'm Edoardo 👋

**AI & Data Science** student at H-FARM College and independent developer building mobile apps, web platforms, and local AI workflows.

#### 🚀 Featured Projects
- **[PhilosophIA](https://philosophia.altervista.org)** — Educational philosophy platform and native Android app (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, on-device Gemini Nano & RAG).
- **QuizCard** — Android study app for flashcards and AI-generated quizzes from documents (Jetpack Compose, Room, Google Drive sync).
- **Finly** — Personal finance and multi-account budgeting Android app with recurring transactions and cloud backup.
- **AuraFlow** — Adaptive AI workout planner that tailors gym routines based on user feedback and progress logs (Flutter & Python).
